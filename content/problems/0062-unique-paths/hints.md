## Hint 1

How many routes reach a cell in the top row? How many reach a cell in the leftmost column?

## Hint 2

For an interior cell, every route must enter either from directly above or from directly to the left. Can you express its count using those two neighbors?

## Hint 3

Fill the grid row by row. Only the previous row matters, so a single row can be overwritten as you move left to right.

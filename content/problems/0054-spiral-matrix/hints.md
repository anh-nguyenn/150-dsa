Track four moving walls: top, bottom, left, and right. Which wall advances after you finish each side of the loop?

## Hint 2

When the unvisited region collapses to one row or one column, which of the four side walks could repeat cells already collected?

## Hint 3

Walk the four sides while the walls have not crossed, and check the walls before the bottom and left walks so a thin region is not read twice.

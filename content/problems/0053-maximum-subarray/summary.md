You are given a non-empty array of integers. Find the largest possible sum of a contiguous slice of the array. A slice is a block of neighboring positions, not a hand-picked subset.

**Example**

```text
nums = [-2, 1, -3, 4, -1, 2, 1, -5, 4]  ->  6
```

The slice `[4, -1, 2, 1]` adds up to `6`, which is the largest sum available.

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 100,000 numbers.
- Each value is between -10,000 and 10,000.
- The slice may not be empty, so the answer can be negative when every value is negative.

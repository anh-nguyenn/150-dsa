Given a string and a budget `k`, you may change at most `k` characters. Return the length of the longest contiguous block that can be made entirely of one character.

**Example**

```text
s = "ABAB", k = 2     ->  4
s = "AABABBA", k = 1  ->  4
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- The string has between 1 and 100,000 characters.
- Characters are uppercase English letters.
- `k` is between 0 and the string length.
- Changing a character means replacing it with any uppercase letter.

Assign either a plus or a minus sign to every number in `nums`, then add the signed values. Count how many assignments produce `target`.

For `nums = [1, 1, 1, 1, 1]` and `target = 3` there are `5` assignments. For `nums = [1]` and `target = 1` there is `1` assignment.

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 20 numbers.
- Each number is between 0 and 1000.
- The target is between -1000 and 1000.
- A zero still has two sign choices even though both contribute `0` to the sum.

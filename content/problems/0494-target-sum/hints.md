## Hint 1

Write the total of the plus-signed numbers as P and the total magnitude of the minus-signed numbers as N. What do P + N and P - N equal?

## Hint 2

The target fixes the sum the plus group must have. When is that required sum impossible to achieve?

## Hint 3

Count subsets with a given sum, scanning each number once and updating totals downward so the same number is not used twice.

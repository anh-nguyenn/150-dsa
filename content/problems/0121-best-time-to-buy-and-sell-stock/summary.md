Given daily stock prices in order, choose one day to buy and a later day to sell, and return the largest possible profit. If no profitable trade exists, return `0`.

**Example**

```text
prices = [7, 1, 5, 3, 6, 4]  ->  5   (buy at 1, sell at 6)
prices = [7, 6, 4, 3, 1]     ->  0
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 100,000 prices.
- Prices are between 0 and 10,000.
- You may complete at most one trade.
- The selling day must come after the buying day.

## Hint 1

Which car reaches the target first if no one blocks anyone, and which direction should you scan to reason about blocking?

## Hint 2

A car behind can catch the car ahead only if its solo arrival time is no larger than the fleet's arrival time. What should you remember as you scan?

## Hint 3

Sort cars by position from nearest the target to farthest, then count a new fleet whenever a car's solo arrival time is greater than the latest fleet time.

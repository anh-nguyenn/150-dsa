Cars travel toward a target on a one-lane road. A faster car that catches a slower car must slow down and join it as one fleet. Count how many fleets reach the target.

```text
target = 12, position = [10,8,0,5,3], speed = [2,4,1,1,3]  ->  3
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 100,000 cars.
- All starting positions are different and lie before the target.
- The target is at most 10^6, and speeds are positive.
- A car that catches another cannot pass it.
- A fleet is one or more cars arriving together.

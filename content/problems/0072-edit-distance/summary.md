Given two strings, return the smallest number of single-character operations needed to turn the first into the second. Allowed operations are inserting a character, deleting a character, and replacing one character with another.

**Example**

```text
word1 = "horse", word2 = "ros"  ->  3
```

One shortest sequence replaces `h` with `r`, removes `r`, and removes `e`.

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Both strings contain only lowercase English letters.
- Each string has at most 500 characters.
- The strings may be empty.

## Hint 1

Which task constrains the schedule most? If one task appears many times, what has to sit between its copies?

## Hint 2

Picture the most frequent task as a row with gaps of length n. How many gaps are there, and what can fill them?

## Hint 3

Let f be the highest frequency and c be the number of tasks with that frequency. The skeleton has (f - 1) blocks of size n + 1 plus c final slots. The answer is the larger of that value and the total task count.

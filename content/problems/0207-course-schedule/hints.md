## Hint 1

Imagine completing courses one at a time. Which courses are available before you have taken anything?

## Hint 2

Taking a course satisfies one requirement for each course that depends on it. What should happen to a course that still has requirements after every available course has been taken?

## Hint 3

Track how many unmet prerequisites each course has and queue the ones at zero. If the queue drains while some courses remain, those courses form or depend on a cycle.

Given a binary tree, decide whether it is height-balanced: at every node, the heights of the left and right subtrees may differ by at most one. An empty tree counts as balanced.

`[3, 9, 20, null, null, 15, 7]` is balanced. `[1, 2, 2, 3, 3, null, null, 4, 4]` is not, because the left subtree of the root is much deeper than the right one.

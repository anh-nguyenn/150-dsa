Each bar in a histogram has width 1 and a given height. Find the area of the largest rectangle that fits entirely inside the histogram.

```text
[2,1,5,6,2,3]  ->  10
[2,4]          ->  4
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 100,000 bars.
- Heights are between 0 and 10,000.
- A rectangle must sit on the baseline and span consecutive bars.
- Its height is limited by the shortest bar it covers.

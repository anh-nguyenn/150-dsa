Return the largest product that any non-empty contiguous subarray can produce.

**Example**

```text
[2, 3, -2, 4]  ->  6    (the subarray [2, 3])
[-2, 0, -1]    ->  0
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 20,000 numbers.
- Each number is between -10 and 10.
- The answer fits in a 32-bit integer.

For each day, report how many days pass until a strictly warmer day. If no warmer day follows, report 0.

```text
[73,74,75,71,69,72,76,73]  ->  [1,1,4,2,1,1,0,0]
[30,60,90]                 ->  [1,1,0]
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 100,000 days.
- Temperatures are between 30 and 100.
- Only a strictly higher temperature counts as warmer.
- The answer for a day with no warmer future day is 0.

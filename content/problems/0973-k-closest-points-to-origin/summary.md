Given points on a two-dimensional plane and a number `k`, return the `k` points closest to the origin. Distance is measured as Euclidean distance, so comparing squared distances is enough. The answer may list the chosen points in any order.

**Example**

```text
points = [[1, 3], [-2, 2]], k = 1
-> [[-2, 2]]
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 10,000 points.
- `k` is between 1 and the number of points.
- Each coordinate is between -10,000 and 10,000.
- Points may repeat.
- Only which points are chosen matters; their order is free.

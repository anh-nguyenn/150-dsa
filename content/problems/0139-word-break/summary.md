Given a string and a dictionary of words, decide whether the string can be split completely into dictionary words. Words may be reused any number of times.

**Example**

```text
s = "leetcode", dictionary = ["leet", "code"]  ->  true
s = "catsandog", dictionary = ["cats", "dog", "sand", "and", "cat"]  ->  false
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- The string has between 1 and 300 characters.
- The dictionary has between 1 and 1,000 words.
- Words are non-empty and consist of lowercase letters.

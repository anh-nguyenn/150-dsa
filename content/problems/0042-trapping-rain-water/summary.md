Given the heights of bars in a row, compute how much rainwater stays trapped between them after it rains. Water above a bar is limited by the shorter of the tallest bars on that bar's left and right.

**Example**

```text
height = [0, 1, 0, 2, 1, 0, 1, 3, 2, 1, 2, 1]  ->  6
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 20,000 bars.
- Heights are between 0 and 100,000.
- Bars have no width, but water collects in the gaps between them.
- Water cannot escape past a taller bar on either side.

## Hint 1

Look at the difference between fuel gained and fuel spent at each station. What does the total of those differences tell you?

## Hint 2

If the tank becomes negative before finishing a lap from a candidate start, can any station between that start and the failure point work instead?

## Hint 3

When the tank goes negative, restart the candidate just after the failing station. Keep a separate total to decide whether any start exists at all.

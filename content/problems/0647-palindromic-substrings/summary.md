Count every contiguous substring of `s` that is a palindrome. Substrings are counted by position, so two identical letters in different places are two separate palindromes.

**Examples**

```text
s = "abc"  ->  3     # "a", "b", "c"
s = "aaa"  ->  6     # three singles, two pairs, one triple
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 1,000 characters.
- The string contains only lowercase English letters.
- A single character is a palindrome.

Reverse the nodes of a linked list in groups of `k`. If the final group has fewer than `k` nodes, leave it in its original order. Change links, not values.

**Examples**

```text
[1, 2, 3, 4, 5], k = 2  ->  [2, 1, 4, 3, 5]
[1, 2, 3, 4, 5], k = 3  ->  [3, 2, 1, 4, 5]
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- The list holds between 1 and 5,000 nodes.
- `k` is between 1 and the number of nodes.
- Values are between 0 and 1,000.
- A leftover group shorter than `k` is not reversed.

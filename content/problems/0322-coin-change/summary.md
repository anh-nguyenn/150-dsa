You have an unlimited supply of coins with given denominations and a target amount. Return the smallest number of coins that adds up to the amount, or `-1` when it cannot be made.

Coins may be reused as often as needed.

**Example**

```text
coins = [1, 2, 5], amount = 11  ->  3   (5 + 5 + 1)
coins = [2],       amount = 3   ->  -1
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 12 coin denominations.
- Each denomination is a positive integer.
- The amount is between 0 and 10,000.

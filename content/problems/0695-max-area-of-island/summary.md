You are given a grid of `0` (water) and `1` (land). The area of an island is its number of land cells, and cells connect up, down, left, and right, but not diagonally. Return the area of the largest island, or `0` when there is no land at all.

**Example**

```text
[[1,1,0],
 [0,0,1],
 [1,0,1]]
```

The largest island has area 2: the pair in the top row and the vertical pair on the right edge both have two cells.

**Constraints, in plain words**

- The grid has at least one row and one column.
- Each cell is either `0` or `1`.
- Connections are only up, down, left, and right.

Vertical lines stand at positions `0` to `n - 1`, each with a height. Choose two lines that, together with the floor, hold the most water. The area is the distance between them times the shorter of their two heights.

**Example**

```text
height = [1, 8, 6, 2, 5, 4, 8, 3, 7]  ->  49
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 2 and 100,000 lines.
- Heights are between 0 and 10,000.
- You must choose two different positions.
- The water level is limited by the shorter line.

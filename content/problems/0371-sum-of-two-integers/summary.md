Add two integers and return their sum without using the `+` or `-` operators.

**Examples**

```text
a = 1, b = 2   ->  3
a = -2, b = 3  ->  1
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Both inputs are between -1,000 and 1,000.
- Inputs may be negative.
- Do not use `+` or `-` anywhere in the solution.
- The result fits in a signed 32-bit integer.

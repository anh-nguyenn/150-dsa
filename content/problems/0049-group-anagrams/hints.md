## Hint 1

All anagrams share something that does not depend on the order of their letters. Can you turn each word into a standard form?

## Hint 2

If two words have the same sorted letter sequence, they belong together. What kind of table could map a standard form to all the words that share it?

## Hint 3

Use the sorted letters as a dictionary key. Append each original word to the bucket for its key, then return all the buckets.

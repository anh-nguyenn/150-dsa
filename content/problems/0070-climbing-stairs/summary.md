A staircase has `n` steps, and each move takes you up either one step or two. Count the different sequences of moves that finish exactly on the top.

**Examples**

```text
n = 2  ->  2      # 1+1 or 2
n = 5  ->  8
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- `1 <= n <= 45`.
- The answer fits in a standard 32-bit signed integer.

Remove the `n`-th node counted from the end of a linked list and return the head of the result. Counting starts at 1, so `n = 1` means the tail.

**Examples**

```text
[1, 2, 3, 4, 5], n = 2  ->  [1, 2, 3, 5]
[1],             n = 1  ->  []
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- The list holds between 1 and 30 nodes.
- `n` is at least 1 and at most the list length.
- Values are between 0 and 100.
- The removed node may be the head or the tail.

Find the length of the longest contiguous substring that has no repeated characters.

**Example**

```text
s = "abcabcbb"  ->  3   ("abc")
s = "bbbbb"     ->  1   ("b")
s = "pwwkew"    ->  3   ("wke")
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- The string has between 0 and 50,000 characters.
- Characters may include letters, digits, symbols, and spaces.
- The substring must be contiguous.
- A substring of length zero is allowed when the string is empty.

A row of houses holds the amounts shown in `nums`. If you rob one house you cannot rob either neighbour, because the alarm links adjacent houses. Return the largest total you can take.

**Examples**

```text
nums = [1, 2, 3, 1]     ->  4      # houses 0 and 2
nums = [2, 7, 9, 3, 1]  ->  12     # houses 0, 2, and 4
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 100 houses.
- Each house holds between 0 and 400.
- Houses sit in a straight line, and adjacent houses cannot both be chosen.

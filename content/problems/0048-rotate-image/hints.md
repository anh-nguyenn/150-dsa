## Hint 1

Trace one cell in a small grid and compare its starting row and column with its ending row and column. What swaps, and what gets reversed?

## Hint 2

A flip across the main diagonal and a horizontal flip are both easy and both work in place. In which order do they combine into a clockwise quarter turn?

## Hint 3

Transpose the grid by swapping cells across the main diagonal, then reverse every row. The result is the clockwise rotation.

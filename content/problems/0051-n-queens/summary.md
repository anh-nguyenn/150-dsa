Place `n` queens on an `n x n` chessboard so that no two queens attack each other. Return every distinct board arrangement. Queens attack along rows, columns, and both diagonals.

**Example**

```text
n = 4
-> [[".Q..", "...Q", "Q...", "..Q."],
    ["..Q.", "Q...", "...Q", ".Q.."]]
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- `n` is between 1 and 9.
- Exactly `n` queens must be placed, one per row and one per column.
- No two queens may share a row, column, or diagonal.
- Each board uses `'Q'` for a queen and `'.'` for an empty square.
- Boards may be returned in any order.

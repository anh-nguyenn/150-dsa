## Hint 1

Two words are connected when they differ in exactly one position. What does the length of a transformation chain measure in that graph?

## Hint 2

Explore the graph in rings: all words one change away, then all words two changes away. Why can you stop the first time the target appears?

## Hint 3

Build the search level by level and avoid revisiting words. Grouping words by the pattern left when one position is wildcarded lets you collect all neighbors at that position without comparing every pair of words.

## Hint 1

List the situations you can be in after a day: holding a share, having just sold, or being free to buy again.

## Hint 2

How does each situation today depend on yesterday's situations? A purchase can only follow a day when you were free.

## Hint 3

Keep three rolling balances for holding, just sold, and free. Compute all three from the previous day's snapshot so a single day is never used twice.

The houses now stand in a circle, so the first and last houses are neighbours. As before, you cannot rob two adjacent houses. Return the largest total from this circular row.

**Examples**

```text
nums = [2, 3, 2]     ->  3      # the two 2s are adjacent through the wrap-around
nums = [1, 2, 3, 1]  ->  4
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Between 1 and 100 houses.
- Each house holds between 0 and 1,000.
- The row is circular: house `0` and house `n - 1` are adjacent.

Find the median of two sorted arrays without fully merging them. For an even total count, the median is the average of the two middle values.

```text
[1,3] and [2]      ->  2.0
[1,2] and [3,4]    ->  2.5
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Each array has between 0 and 1,000 numbers.
- Together they contain at least one number.
- Both arrays are sorted ascending.
- Values lie between -10^6 and 10^6.
- The result is a real number, compared with a small tolerance.

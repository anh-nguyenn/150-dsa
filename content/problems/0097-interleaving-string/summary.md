Given three strings `s1`, `s2`, and `s3`, decide whether `s3` is an interleaving of the first two. An interleaving reads every character of `s1` and `s2` in its original order, choosing at each step from the front of either string.

For `s1 = "aabcc"`, `s2 = "dbbca"`, and `s3 = "aadbbcbcac"` the answer is `true`. Changing `s3` to `"aadbbbaccc"` makes it `false`.

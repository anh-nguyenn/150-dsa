Design a `MedianFinder` class that accepts numbers one at a time and reports the median of all numbers seen so far. For an odd count the median is the middle value; for an even count it is the average of the two middle values.

**Example**

```text
addNum(1)
addNum(2)
findMedian()  ->  1.5
addNum(3)
findMedian()  ->  2.0
```

**Constraints, in plain words**

- Each number is between -100,000 and 100,000.
- At most 50,000 operations are performed.
- `findMedian` is only called after at least one number has been added.
- Results are floating-point values with small tolerance for precision.
